I often (understandably) get asked about my name, which is already peculiar in the Netherlands and even more so internationally — so here's a short explanation:

- My first name is "Han" (formally Johannes), and my last name is "van der Aa".
- "Aa" is a river name — there are actually [many rivers with that name](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liste_der_Gew%C3%A4sser_namens_Aa) across the Netherlands and Germany — so the surname means "from the river Aa".
- Fun fact: in the Netherlands, the [_tussenvoegsel_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tussenvoegsel) (name prefix, e.g., "van der") is not considered when sorting surnames alphabetically. That makes it hard to beat me in any list sorted by last name.
