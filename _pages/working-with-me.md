I am always happy to hear from students, researchers, and organizations who share an interest in process mining and related topics. Below you can find how best to reach out, depending on what you have in mind.

- **Open positions:** Funded PhD and postdoc positions are advertised on the [University of Vienna job portal](https://jobs.univie.ac.at/search/?createNewAlert=false&q=%22workflow+systems+and+technology%22&optionsFacetsDD_department=&optionsFacetsDD_customfield3=&optionsFacetsDD_customfield2=&optionsFacetsDD_facility=) and on my [LinkedIn page](https://www.linkedin.com/in/hanvanderaa/). In Austria, PhD candidates are typically employed on funded positions, so I can only take on new PhD candidates when funding is available, and the number of positions is limited. Please apply through the advertised positions rather than sending unsolicited applications.
- **Theses and projects (University of Vienna students):** For bachelor's and master's theses and P1/P2 projects, please see the [open topics and application information](https://wst.cs.univie.ac.at/teaching/open-topics/) on the WST webpage.
- **Visiting researchers:** Researchers and PhD candidates interested in a research stay with my group are very welcome. Please send me a brief description of your research interests, the intended period of your stay, and how it would be funded (e.g., Erasmus+ or a scholarship from your home institution).
- **External PhD candidates:** If you are interested in pursuing an external PhD on process mining or related topics, for instance while employed in industry, please send me your CV and a short outline of your intended research direction. External PhDs also require secured funding, typically through your employer.
- **Industry partners:** Organizations interested in joint research, such as applying process mining techniques to their data or co-supervising theses, are welcome to get in touch.

Please note that I receive more requests than I can accommodate, so I cannot respond to generic or mass emails.
